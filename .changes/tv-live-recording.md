TV mode can now record live TV: press Record (gamepad RT, keyboard R) to
capture the current channel to disk, and browse finished recordings from the
new Recordings panel (gamepad left-stick click, keyboard L). Manual stop only
in this release — scheduling a recording in advance isn't supported yet.
Only direct MPEG-TS streams can be recorded, not HLS or DASH.
